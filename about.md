# About Me

Hi, I'm **Manas Goth**.

I am an **Applied Data Science Advisor** with over a decade of experience helping organizations leverage data, analytics, and machine learning to solve complex business problems. My work has primarily focused on customer intelligence, predictive analytics, logistics networks, and AI-driven decision making within large-scale global operations.

Throughout my career, I have worked at the intersection of business strategy and technology—building customer analytics solutions, developing machine learning models, leading analytics initiatives, and translating technical insights into measurable business impact. My expertise spans data science, machine learning, MLOps, customer analytics, and the rapidly evolving field of Generative AI.

This blog is a reflection of my continuous learning journey.

As I deepen my expertise in PyTorch, Deep Learning, Transformers, Large Language Models, and AI Engineering, I want to document not only *what* I learn, but also *how* I learn it. My goal is to explore concepts from first principles, understand the mathematics that drive them, implement them from scratch, and connect them to real-world industry applications.

I firmly believe that one of the biggest challenges for aspiring data scientists and AI engineers is bridging the gap between academic knowledge and practical industry expectations. Through these articles, tutorials, and projects, I hope to make that transition easier by combining theory, implementation, business context, and lessons learned from real-world experience.

Whether you're a student, a working professional, or an experienced practitioner looking to deepen your understanding, I hope you find something here that helps accelerate your own learning journey.

Welcome aboard.
